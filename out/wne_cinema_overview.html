How to read these

  • Filled dot = observed, hollow = generated. Frames between observations are interpolated for smooth playback and are never data.
  • Borrowed months say so. Gap-filled frames read “image from <year>”. Runs ending _nopool contain no borrowed frames — use those for plotting.
  • Grey is absence, not zero. Cloud, shadow and failed thermal retrievals are painted neutral grey and never filled in.
  • Colour scales are fixed per video, so a colour means the same thing in every frame. The same data on two different ramps can look completely different — compare only within a video.
  • Thermal methods differ. The USGS product and the mono-window retrieval typically sit 1–3 K apart; do not mix figures from the two.
